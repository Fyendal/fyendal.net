# Third-party audio

The files in `cards/` are renamed, otherwise unmodified selections from
Kenney's **Casino Audio 1.1** pack:

- `draw-1.ogg` through `draw-3.ogg`: `card-slide-1.ogg` through
  `card-slide-3.ogg`
- `play-1.ogg` and `play-2.ogg`: `card-place-1.ogg` and `card-place-2.ogg`
- `shuffle.ogg`: `card-shuffle.ogg`

Source: <https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio>  
Creator: Kenney Vleugels (Kenney.nl)  
License: [Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal (CC0 1.0)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)  
Retrieved: 2026-08-30

The source pack states that the assets may be used in personal and commercial
projects and that attribution is appreciated but not required.
